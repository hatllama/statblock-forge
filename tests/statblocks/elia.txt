ELIA DANTES
Medium humanoid (human, minotaur heritage), any alignment

Armor Class 16 (studded leather)
Hit Points 165 (22d8 + 66)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 14 (+2)  DEX 18 (+4)  CON 16 (+3)  INT 12 (+1)  WIS 14 (+2)  CHA 14 (+2)

Saving Throws Dex +7, Con +6
Skills Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +5, Stealth +7, Survival +5
Senses passive Perception 15
Languages Common
Challenge 7 (2,900 XP)    Proficiency Bonus +3

Masked. Elia wears a plain mask and plain clothes. Nothing on her names the Family.

First Stake. Elia's luck works through the holy coin she was given at her Staking, which must be on her person. Without it she loses every luck ability.

Trained Shooter. Elia ignores the loading property of crossbows.

Tight Grouping. Once per turn, when Elia hits the same creature with two or more attacks, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) piercing damage.

Chain Bolts. Elia carries twelve chain bolts. A creature hit by a chain bolt has its speed halved until the end of its next turn.

Push Your Luck. When Elia misses with an attack, she can reroll it and must use the new result. She takes 2 (1d4) damage each time she does.

ACTIONS

Multiattack. Elia makes four carabina attacks. One of them can be a burst.

Carabina. Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

Burst. Elia expends three pieces of ammunition for one carabina attack. On a hit, it deals 17 (3d8 + 4) piercing damage.

Brass Knuckles. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
