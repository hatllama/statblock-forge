DORIAN VECK
Medium humanoid (human), any alignment

Armor Class 16 (glassmaker's leathers)
Hit Points 71 (11d8 + 22)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 12 (+1)  DEX 18 (+4)  CON 14 (+2)  INT 14 (+2)  WIS 12 (+1)  CHA 12 (+1)

Skills Perception +4, Sleight of Hand +7
Tool Proficiencies glassblower's tools
Senses passive Perception 14
Languages Common
Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)    Proficiency Bonus +3

Borrowed Greed. Dorian's power is a fragment of Osric's loan. He
understands what it does, if not how to master it, and he uses it
carefully.

ACTIONS
Multiattack. Dorian makes two attacks, using Blowpipe or Molten Gather
in any combination.

Blowpipe. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) fire damage. His iron blowpipe comes straight from
the furnace, glowing at the end.

Molten Gather. Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 20/40 ft., one
target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) fire damage, and the target's speed is
reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn as the glass hardens
on it.

Furnace Flare (Recharge 5-6). Dorian swings his blowpipe and throws out
a spray of molten glass in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone
must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) fire damage on
a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

BONUS ACTIONS
Grasping Want (1/Day). Dorian turns two creatures he can see within 30
feet of him, and within reach of each other, against each other's
belongings. Each must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or use
its action on its next turn trying to take an object the other is
holding or wearing.

REACTIONS
Careful Distance. When a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of Dorian,
he moves up to 10 feet away from it without provoking opportunity
attacks.

Molten Screen. When Dorian is hit by an attack, he throws up a pane of
hot glass and adds 2 to his AC against that attack, possibly causing it
to miss.
