OSRIC ALDERMARCH
Medium humanoid (human), any alignment

Armor Class 16 (glass-laced coat)
Hit Points 157 (21d8 + 63)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 10 (+0)  DEX 14 (+2)  CON 16 (+3)  INT 18 (+4)  WIS 16 (+3)  CHA 20 (+5)

Saving Throws Con +8, Int +9, Wis +8, Cha +10
Skills Deception +10, Insight +8, Perception +8, Persuasion +10
Senses passive Perception 18
Languages Common, Infernal
Challenge 15 (13,000 XP)    Proficiency Bonus +5

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If Osric fails a saving throw, he can
choose to succeed instead.

Burning Through. Everything below comes from Ossendrel's loan. When
Osric uses it, the lustre of his palms burns straight through his
gloves — and every use sinks him a little deeper into his debt.

ACTIONS
Multiattack. Osric makes two Gilded Shard attacks.

Gilded Shard. Ranged Attack: +10 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target.
Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) fire damage. His wealth
tears out of his palm as a burning, gold-veined shard.

Ruinous Fortune (Recharge 5-6). A fortune in burning, gold-veined
shards pours down on a 20-foot-radius sphere centred on a point Osric
can see within 120 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 19
Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) slashing damage on a failed
save, or half as much on a successful one. The area is left littered
with broken wealth and becomes difficult terrain until it's swept clear.

BONUS ACTIONS
Covetous Glint. One creature Osric can see within 30 feet must succeed
on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or spend its movement on its next turn
moving toward the nearest valuable object or creature carrying one, and
trying to take it.

REACTIONS
Bought Off. When a creature Osric can see makes an attack roll against
him, he offers it everything it's ever wanted. It must succeed on a
DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or make the attack with disadvantage.

LEGENDARY ACTIONS
Osric can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below.
Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of
another creature's turn. He regains spent legendary actions at the start
of his turn.

Shard. Osric makes one Gilded Shard attack.

Withdraw. Osric moves up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity
attacks.

Gilded Weight (Costs 2 Actions). Gold-veined crust creeps over up to two
creatures Osric can see within 60 feet. Each must succeed on a DC 19
Strength saving throw or, until the end of its next turn, have its
speed halved and be unable to take reactions.

LAIR ACTIONS
Anywhere in Aldermarch Hall, on initiative count 20 (losing initiative
ties), Osric can take one lair action. He can't use the same lair action
two rounds in a row.

Shattering Display. The house's display bursts — a glass sculpture, a
chandelier, a case of fine pieces — over a 20-foot square Osric can see.
Each creature there must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 16
(3d10) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful
one. The square becomes difficult terrain.

The Wards Wake. One creature Osric can see must succeed on a DC 18
Charisma saving throw or be compelled to steal for 1 minute, as though
it had crossed one of the Hall's wards.

Everything Has a Price. The wealth on every wall calls out. Each
creature of Osric's choice in the room must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom
saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls against him until
initiative count 20 on the next round.

The House Answers. 1d4 household guards (see The Lastlecross Watch)
arrive at the entrances of the room at initiative count 0.
