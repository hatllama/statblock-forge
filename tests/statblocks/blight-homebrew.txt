Stagnation Blight of the Arbiter
Large elemental, lawful neutral
Armor Class 14
Hit Points 52 (7d10 + 14)
Speed 30 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
14 (+2) 12 (+1) 14 (+2) 8 (-1) 12 (+1) 6 (-2)
Damage Resistances force, radiant
Condition Immunities frightened, charmed
Senses blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 11
Languages understands Primordial but can't speak
Challenge 3 (700 XP)
Edict of Stillness. Any creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of the blight without having moved at least 15 feet during that turn takes 7 (2d6) force damage.
Death Throes. When the blight dies, it bursts. Each creature within 10 feet of it must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
Actions
Multiattack. The blight makes two Force Lash attacks.
Force Lash. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) force damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
Edict of Forced Relocation (Recharge 4–6). The blight targets up to two creatures it can see within 30 feet. Each target must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be restrained until the end of its next turn.
Reactions
Inertia Punishment. When a creature within 10 feet of the blight moves, the blight can force it to make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) force damage on a failed save.
