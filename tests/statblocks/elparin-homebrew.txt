Calculated Defense. Elparin adds his Intelligence modifier (+8) to his Armor Class. He relies on spatial geometry and predictive timing
rather than martial skill or magic armor.
Unfit for Violence. Elparin possesses no innate martial prowess or standard magical spellcasting. He cannot make opportunity attacks, and
his physical attacks deal no non-supernatural damage. However, his mastery of constitutional reality alters how mechanics function for
everyone around him.
Encyclopedic Jurisprudence. Elparin draws upon legal concepts learned across the continent, applying distinct passive fields based on
regional precedents:
• Reultran Economic Law: Whenever a creature within 60 feet expends a spell slot, superiority die, or daily resource, it must pay a
"transaction tax" of 10 force damage unless it expends its movement to pay the fee.
• Majd Emotional Custom: Whenever a creature within 60 feet deals critical hit damage or fails a saving throw by 5 or more, the emotional
outburst triggers a civil penalty, forcing the target to make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of Elparin until the end of
its next turn.
• Ters Ursel Rebuilding Charter: Creatures within 30 feet of Elparin cannot regain hit points or gain temporary hit points while standing on
broken, damaged, or altered terrain.

Actions
Legislative Revision. Elparin makes two Statutory Editing strikes or uses Expunge Clause once.
Statutory Editing. Ranged Spell Attack: +14 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d8 + 8) psychic damage. Elparin cites a fundamental
flaw in the target's physical logic, forcing the target to make a DC 22 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, Elparin alters one key statistic
on the target (AC, movement speed, or a single saving throw modifier) by 3 until the start of his next turn.
Expunge Clause (Recharge 5–6). Elparin selects one established rule of combat or magic within 90 feet and temporarily erases it from
existence until the start of his next turn. Choose one option:
• Suspension of Gravity: Fall damage, flying restrictions, and ground movement mechanics cease to exist. All creatures float helplessly in
place; ground movement is 0 ft., and attack rolls have disadvantage.
• Suspension of Immunity: All damage immunities and resistances across all creatures within 90 feet vanish completely.
• Suspension of Causality: Spells and attacks made this turn deal zero immediate damage. Instead, all accumulated damage is delayed
and dealt all at once at the start of Elparin's next turn as pure psychic damage.
Constitutional Amendment (3/Day). Elparin rewrites an active spell, ability, or mechanic currently affecting the battlefield. He can change a
spell's damage type, swap the required saving throw statistic of an ongoing effect to Intelligence, or change the target of a concentration
spell to another creature within range.

Reactions
Stay of Execution. When Elparin or an ally within 60 feet takes damage that would reduce them to 0 hit points, Elparin taps his journal and
issues an immediate injunction. The damage is momentarily erased from the record, leaving the target at 1 hit point until the end of the
next turn, at which point the clause reappears.
Procedural Nullification. When a creature within 60 feet targets Elparin with an attack or spell, Elparin cites an illegal entry in the action.
The attacker must succeed on a DC 22 Intelligence saving throw or the action is canceled and the attacker's action is wasted.

Legendary Actions
Elparin can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time and only at the end of another
creature's turn. Elparin regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.
Cite Precedent. Elparin forces one creature within 60 feet to make a DC 22 History or Intelligence check. On a failure, the creature is
overwhelmed by legal paradoxes and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.
Redact Position (Costs 2 Actions). Elparin temporarily erases the spatial coordinates of one creature within 60 feet. The target vanishes
from the battlefield until the start of its turn, reappearing in an unoccupied space of Elparin's choosing within 30 feet of its original position.
Draft Injunction (Costs 3 Actions). Elparin declares a specific action type (e.g., "Casting spells of 3rd level or higher," "Making melee
attacks," or "Using bonus actions") illegal. Until the end of his next turn, any creature that performs that action takes 33 (6d10) psychic
damage.
