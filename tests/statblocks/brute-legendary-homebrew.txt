Grukk, the Mountain's Fist
Huge giant, chaotic evil

A hill giant chieftain who has eaten three rivals and wears their belts. No spells, no tricks: legendary actions and a thick skull.

Armor Class 15 (natural armor)
Hit Points 170 (16d12 + 64)
Speed 40 ft.
STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
23 (+6) 9 (-1) 19 (+4) 7 (-2) 10 (+0) 11 (+0)
Saving Throws Str +10, Con +8
Senses passive Perception 10
Languages Giant
Challenge 9 (5,000 XP)
Proficiency Bonus +4
Legendary Resistance (2/Day). If Grukk fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
Actions
Multiattack. Grukk makes two Greatclub attacks.
Greatclub. Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
Boulder. Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
Legendary Actions
Grukk can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Grukk regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
Club. Grukk makes one Greatclub attack.
Ground Slam (Costs 2 Actions). Each creature within 10 feet of Grukk must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 16 (3d10) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
Bellow (Costs 2 Actions). Each creature of Grukk's choice within 30 feet that can hear it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of its next turn.
