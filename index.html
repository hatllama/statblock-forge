The new AI plays fighters and full casters (wizards, clerics, druids, bards, sorcerers, warlocks, spellcasting monsters); paladins, rangers and other part-casters stay classic for now. For the party that is the tactical setting; monsters need to be on the tactical AI.

Learning keeps notes on every roll, so running the odds takes a little longer.

How the knowledge settings workOnly creatures on the tactical AI use them. Black box guesses hit points, AC and saves from size, and doesn't know resistances or how many fights are left. Learning starts the same, then narrows AC from hits and misses, saves from what's made and failed, and hit points from what bloodies and drops a creature. It notes resistances, healers and area attacks, and remembers each kind of creature for the rest of the day.

Forge a statblock

Paste one or more statblocks: Monster Manual, D&D Beyond, 5etools, Homebrewery or GM Binder markdown, a homebrew doc. Separate several with a blank line or a line of dashes (---).

Read right here in your browser. HP, AC, saves, speeds, attacks, multiattacks, breath weapons and other save effects, damage types, recharge and limited uses, reactions, legendary and lair actions, resistances, regeneration and spellcasting all come across. Anything it had to guess or leave out is listed as a parser note on the creature. Review it before relying on it: it's a strong first draft, not gospel.
